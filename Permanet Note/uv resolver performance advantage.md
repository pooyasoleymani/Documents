uv's Rust-based dependency resolver completes installs ~5x faster than pip's resolver—critical for [[CI-CD pipeline speed]] and reducing [[developer wait time]] during environment setup.

## Why it matters:
Faster resolution shrinks feedback loops in test-driven development, directly impacting [[team velocity]].
Fast resolvers like uv make [[scikit-build-core CMake-Python bridge]] practical in tight [[CI-CD pipeline speed]] windows.
