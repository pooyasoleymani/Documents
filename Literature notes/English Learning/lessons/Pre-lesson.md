# Grammar You Need to Learn

From your writing, I noticed these recurring issues:

1. **Capitalization**
    - `i` → `I`
    - `english` → `English`
    - `iran` → `Iran`
2. **Articles (`a`, `an`, `the`)**
    - `I have bachelor`
    - ✔️ `I have a Bachelor's degree`
3. **Verb tenses**
    - `I recently start`
    - ✔️ `I recently started`
4. **Prepositions**
    - `work in`
    - ✔️ `work for`
    - `live in Tehran`
5. **Natural expressions**
    - `Tehran city`
    - ✔️ `Tehran`
6. **Sentence linking**  
    Instead of many short sentences:

> I am a programmer. I learn Go. I work...

Use connectors:

> I am a programmer, and I recently started learning Go.
