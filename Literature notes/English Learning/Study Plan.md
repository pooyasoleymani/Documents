## What Band 6.5 Means

A Band 6.5 student can generally:

- Understand most everyday and academic English.
- Speak with reasonable fluency.
- Write organized essays and reports.
- Read articles and technical documents.
- Understand conversations and lectures with only occasional difficulty.
- Make grammatical mistakes, but not so many that they interfere with communication.

# How I'll Teach You

I won't just answer questions.

I'll act like a teacher.

For example:

### Reading

I'll give you an article.

Then ask questions.

Then explain difficult vocabulary.

Then teach grammar from the article.

---

### Speaking

I'll ask IELTS speaking questions like an examiner.

Example:

> Describe a difficult problem you solved.

You answer.

Then I'll correct:

- grammar
- vocabulary
- pronunciation (using phonetic explanations when helpful)
- natural expressions
- IELTS band estimate

---

### Writing

You write an essay.

I'll mark it exactly like an IELTS examiner:

- Task Response
- Coherence & Cohesion
- Lexical Resource
- Grammar Range & Accuracy

Then I'll rewrite it into a Band 7+ version and explain why it's stronger.

---

### Listening

I'll recommend short listening exercises.

Afterward, we'll review:

- new vocabulary
- sentence structures
- connected speech
- why certain words were difficult to catch

5. Modal verbs
6. Relative clauses
7. Conditionals
8. Reported speech

# Vocabulary

Instead of memorizing long word lists, we'll learn vocabulary in context.

For example, if today's topic is programming:

Instead of learning:

> efficient

You'll learn:

- efficient algorithm
- efficient memory usage
- efficient implementation
- computational efficiency

Then you'll write your own sentences using those expressions.

This is much closer to how IELTS vocabulary is assessed.
