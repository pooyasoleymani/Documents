

**Object-oriented programming** is a paradigm based on the concept of wrapping pieces of data,and behavior related to that data, into special bundles called objects, which are construct
ed from a set of “blueprints”, defined by a programmer, called classes.

![[oop001.png]]


> [!NOTE] 
> Data stored inside the object’s fields is often referenced as state, and all the object’s methods define its behavior.


## Class hierarchies

a real program contains more than a single class. Some of these classes might be organized into *class hierarchies*. Let’s find out what that means.

A parent class, like the one we’ve just defined, is called a *superclass*. Its children are *subclasses*. *Subclasses* inherit state and behavior from their parent, defining only attributes or
behaviors that differ.

![[oop002.png]]

*Subclasses* can ***override*** the behavior of methods that they inherit from *parent* classes.
